How I test

I test only with your authorization and under rules agreed in advance. The details of each engagement are settled in the offer and the contract.

Authorization

I test only with a written order, and only systems that you own or are permitted to have tested.

Before the test we agree in writing on scope, test type (black, grey or white box) and time window.

During the test

I report critical findings to you immediately, not only in the report.

AI in the penetration test

AI agents handle reconnaissance and breadth: they map the attack surface and run broad, automated tests. I set the agreed scope as a fixed boundary for them and supervise their work. I follow up exploitable findings by hand and verify every finding in the report myself.

I pass data from your engagement to an AI model, Anthropic Claude, only with your written consent. Without it I work without cloud AI.

Standards and credentials

I follow the BSI penetration testing guideline (IS-Pentest-Leitfaden) and the OWASP Web Security Testing Guide (WSTG).

My certifications: OSCP and SCP Web Application Security.

I hold professional liability insurance with exali.
