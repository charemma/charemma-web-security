Agents map it. I go in.

AI agents gather in parallel what is publicly discoverable about your systems. Then I take over: targeted, manual and all the way to a verifiable proof. The result is a report your team can act on right away.

03 / process

From scoping to retest

  1. 01

    Scoping

    Goals, scope, test type (black, grey or white box) and time window agreed in writing.

  2. 02

    Testing

    AI-assisted reconnaissance, then manual testing. Critical findings are reported immediately, not just in the report.

  3. 03

    Report

    Management summary plus technical findings with reproduction steps, PoC and a concrete fix.

  4. 04

    Retest

    After your fixes I test again and confirm the remediation in writing.

Knowledge 05 / ai-security

More than prompt injection

AI applications inherit the weaknesses of classic software and add their own. Here I explain the most important risks for LLM applications and AI pipelines – briefly, clearly and with a link to the in-depth article.

LLM applications

Risks in applications built on language models
  • KnowledgeLLM01:2026

    Prompt injection

    Inputs – typed directly or hidden in documents, web pages and emails – make a model ignore its instructions or follow someone else’s.

    Read more →
  • KnowledgeLLM03:2026

    Excessive agency

    An agent can do more than its task requires: too many tools, too broad permissions, too little human confirmation.

    Read more →
  • KnowledgeLLM05:2026

    Data and model poisoning

    Manipulated training, fine-tuning or embedding data deliberately change how a model behaves.

    Read more →
